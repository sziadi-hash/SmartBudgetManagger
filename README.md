# SmartBudgetManagger
What it does
SmartBudget Manager is an Excel workbook that helps you track your monthly income and expenses. It
calculates your balance automatically, shows a chart of expenses by category, and warns you when you
go over your budget.
What you need
• Microsoft Excel 2016 or later
• The file SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx
• Basic Excel skills (open a file, click a cell, type)
The three worksheets
• Dashboard: shows your total income, total expenses, balance, budget status and chart
• Transactions: where you enter your income and expenses
• Categories: the expense categories and your monthly budget for each one
Getting started
1. Open SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx.
2. Open the Categories worksheet and set your monthly budget for each category.
3. Open the Transactions worksheet. In the first empty row, enter the date, description, type,
category and amount. Choose the type and category from the drop-down lists.
4. Press Enter.
5. Open the Dashboard worksheet to see your balance, status and chart.
6. Save the file with Ctrl + S.
